# Tidewrought - implementation plan

Faction 5 of `claude/design-factions.md`: corsairs and tide-priests; shipwreck harbours, brass, kelp, coral; teal and
brass; **"they bring the sea with them"**. BAR role: naval + hover play, but it **must work on maps with no water**.
Signature: **Tides** - Tidewrought units are faster and regenerate on water or on **tidepools** (temporary shallow
water a few blocks wide, ~30 s) that Tide Priests and the Harbourmaster raise anywhere; enemies can drain or fill them.

This plan reuses the template of `design/verdant_court_plan.md` (section 1 there lists every faction touch point and
is not repeated here) and the lessons the Court taught. Paths are relative to
`bar/src/main/java/com/solegendary/reignofnether/` unless they start with `bar/`, `tools/` or `design/`.

---

## 1. What the Court already solved (do it the same way)

| Lesson from the Court | What the Tidewrought does |
|---|---|
| Faction look/mechanics live in one `faction/FactionTraits` entry; no faction may fall through to Sunforged | `FactionTraits.TIDEWROUGHT` exists from slice 0 (teal and brass livery, cyan/copper banner, mangrove/prismarine scaffold, dark-prismarine wreck). Slice 1 fills `dgunKind`, `commanderAbility`, `fx`, `extractor`, `ambience` and adds a `tides` flag. |
| Register after NONE so registry ids never move | Registered right after `VERDANT_COURT` (slice 0, GameTest checks the order). |
| `Factions.isLive` / `randomLive` drive "Random" | Nothing to do: the faction joins the pool the moment slice 1 drops `setPreview()`. |
| Skirmish screen + packet are int-coded (`3` = random, Verdant = `4`) | Tidewrought takes **`5`** (comment reserved in `SkirmishServerboundPacket.factionOf`). Add `"Tidewrought"` to `SkirmishSetupScreen.FACTIONS` at index 5 in slice 1. Never reuse 3. |
| `BotPlayer.kitFor` falls back to the villager kit | Slice 1 adds the kit in the same commit that makes the faction live; `/rts bot` parse gets `"tidewrought", "tide"`. `startRTSBot` now refuses preview factions (slice 0). |
| Structures go in **both** `assets/` and `data/` trees, generated by `tools/gen_structures.py` | Add a `"_tide"` palette (section 5). Revert unchanged NBT after regenerating. |
| New buildings need `building/BuildingSaveData` cases | One case per building in the same commit as the building. |
| Prefer extending the vanilla/AM mob + copying Unit boilerplate over extending a faction's unit class (`instanceof VillagerUnit / IronGolemUnit` checks) | Same rule. Explicitly: never extend `DrownedUnit` (Gravebound night healing, `MobilityClass` instanceof Drowned, wreck rules). |
| Death-FX faction field is 3 bits (`BarFx.F_*`, room for three more) | `F_TIDE = 5`: brass shards, teal spray, a splash puff, vanilla `SPLASH` / `BUBBLE_POP` particles. `N_TIDE = 5` nanolathe tint. |
| Alex's Mobs bodies by **extending the AM entity class** and registering AM's renderer (Stag Lancer = `EntityMoose` + `RenderMoose`) | Same pattern (section 4). AM is a hard dependency now (`compat/AlexsMobsCompat`, `bar/build.gradle`). |
| Lobby width: each tile costs the name column 22 px | Six tiles from slice 0. Seven factions + random = eight tiles: switch to a dropdown/second row before faction 6 is announced. |

lifetime (and its scheduled-tick fail-safe removes it with the registry empty, simulating a reload); the per-player
cap evicts the owner's oldest pool; a Tidewrought unit on a tidepool regenerates and gets the speed modifier while an
enemy on the same cell does not, and the modifier is removed on leaving; a Tidepool cell classifies as
`KIND_TIDEPOOL` and is walkable for every mobility class; the four-live-factions test grows to five.

### Slice 4 - T3 (M)
T3 lab **Abyssal Shipyard** (needs Tier 3 + a Harbourmaster as builder, `T2Workers.isT3Lab`, `ResearchTier3`).
**The Leviathan**: AM `EntityLobster` scaled ~5x (crab-beast) with a fortress render layer; extend `EntityLobster`.
`EntityLobster` is a `WaterAnimal` - override air supply / `canBreatheUnderwater`, and `MobilityClass.of` must return
`TIDAL` before its `instanceof WaterAnimal -> AQUATIC` check (section 6). Walks slow on land (0.12), swims fast
(water multiplier 2.5), leaves trail tidepools (r1, 10 s, own cap 6) every 3 blocks moved, *Tidal Slam* (wide arc
knockback + 20 dmg, 40 s cooldown, telegraphed 1 s). Weakness per the T3 rules: no anti-air on the fortress, slow
on land, draining its trail strands escorts. ~5x a T2 heavy in cost.

## 3. Tidepool implementation (slice 1 core)

### 3.1 It is NOT vanilla water
A real water source on flat ground spreads 7 blocks, turns lava into obsidian/cobblestone, hydrates farmland,
hardens concrete powder, puts out fires permanently, is bucket-pickupable, and makes units swim, bob and drown.
That is permanent terrain damage and uncontrollable physics. So:

**`blocks/TidepoolBlock`**: a custom block with **no fluid state** (`getFluidState` = empty), no collision, no
occlusion, `canBeReplaced = true` (buildings and players placing blocks simply overwrite it), `MapColor.WATER`,
translucent render type, model = a 2/16-high sheet using the vanilla water still texture tinted teal + a foam edge,
bubble/splash particles in `animateTick` (client, random-tick-free). Blockstate `AGE` 0-3 for the drying-out look.
The "wet" effects are coded explicitly instead of inherited:
- Tidewrought units on it: speed modifier + regen (3.3).
- Enemy units: nothing in slice 1 (counterplay is draining it, not slowing). A wading slow is a tuning knob.
- Extinguishes burning entities standing in it (`clearFire`), so the Horde's fire matters.

Real water (`FluidTags.WATER` at feet) gives Tidewrought the same bonus, so naval maps reward them extra.

### 3.2 Placement and cleanup (no permanent terrain damage)
- Raised as a disc (Tide Priest r2 = 13 cells, Harbourmaster r3 = 29, Leviathan trail r1 = 5) at the surface: for
  each column, the first **air** cell above a block with a sturdy top face, within +-2 of the aimed y. Never replaces
  anything (not grass, flowers, snow layers, crops), never inside a building footprint
  (`BuildingUtils.isPosInsideAnyBuilding`), never on a metal patch, never under another tidepool.
- **Pool registry** `tide/TidepoolServerEvents`: one record per pool `{owner, centre, long[] cells, expireTick}`.
  Expiry checked once per second, not per tick. On expiry each cell is removed **only if it is still a
  TidepoolBlock** (a building or another block may have replaced it).
- **Fail-safe that survives a crash/reload**: each block schedules a block tick for `lifetime + 5 s` on placement.
  Scheduled block ticks are saved with the chunk, so a pool whose registry entry was lost (server restart, chunk
  unloaded at expiry) still removes itself; `onLoad` of a chunk never resurrects water. The registry is not saved.
- Match end / `/rts reset` / player defeat: the owner's pools are cleared at once.
- Enemies can **drain** a pool: any enemy constructor's reclaim command on a tidepool cell drains the whole pool
  in 2 s (no resources); fire-typed attacks (Blaze, Wildfire, Magma Rupture, Sunfire/Bloodstorm D-guns) evaporate
  the cells they hit; and **fill** it by placing any building over it (replaceable).

### 3.3 Tides buff (performance at 8v8)
`tide/TidesServerEvents`: every **10 ticks**, for each live Tidewrought unit (iterate the existing per-player unit
lists, no entity search, no allocation): `wet = level.getBlockState(feet).is(TIDEPOOL) || feetFluid.is(WATER)`.
Only on a **change** of wet state add/remove one `AttributeModifier` (fixed UUID, +25% speed, `TRANSIENT`); regen
1 HP/s (Reef Guard 3) applied as 0.5/1.5 HP per 10 ticks. No packets: speed syncs through attributes, health through
vanilla. `FactionTraits.tides` gates the whole thing (no `Factions.TIDEWROUGHT ==` checks in the hot loop).

### 3.4 Caps (8v8 = 16 players)

|---|---|---|

### 3.5 RoN pathing and fog
- **Pathing**: `WalkabilityBuilder.classify` checks `TidepoolBlock` at feet **before** the fluid checks and returns a
  new `KIND_TIDEPOOL = 6` (the kind is a byte; check every `switch (kind)` in `pathfinding/` and `PathConverter`
  maps it to `BlockPathTypes.WALKABLE`). `MobilityClass.costFor`: `KIND_TIDEPOOL` = 1.0 for `TIDAL`, 1.5 for others
  (a mild "wading" preference to go round, never a wall). Because a tidepool is walkable for everyone, the dirty-chunk
  reclassify lag (`MAX_CHUNK_RECLASSIFY_PER_TICK = 8`, ~2 s settle ceiling) can never strand or misroute a unit:
  stale cells read as land, which is also walkable. A raise touches at most 4 chunks.
- **New mobility class `TIDAL`** (all Tidewrought ground units): land 1.0, tidepool 1.0, real water 1.5 (they swim
  well; AQUATIC is 3.0, everyone else 25). Footprint is independent of the class (`ChunkSnapshot.footprintRadius`),
  so the Land-Galleon and Leviathan reuse `TIDAL` with a wider footprint - no `TIDAL_LARGE` needed. In
  `MobilityClass.of` the Tidewrought check (via `FactionTraits.tides` of the unit's faction or a `TidalUnit` marker
  interface) goes **first**, before Strider/Drowned/`WaterAnimal`/width checks.
- **Fog of war**: tidepools are blocks, so they ride the existing fog-filtered block updates
  (`FogOfWarServerEvents.shouldSendChunkPacket`): an enemy only sees a pool raised in a visible cell. A pool that
  expires inside a frozen (dark) chunk stays drawn on that enemy's client until the chunk is seen again - the same
  "last seen" behaviour RoN gives buildings; acceptable. Minimap shows pools as water colour through `MapColor`.
- No new packets for slice 1 beyond the ability's existing aimed-cast packet.

## 6. Naval / water pathing risks in RoN

- **Water costs**: non-aquatic classes pay 25 per water cell, so today every army avoids lakes. Tidewrought gets
  `TIDAL` (1.5); enemies keep 25, so a Tidewrought army can cross water an enemy can't follow quickly - intended,
  matches BAR naval, but watch for bots/units that "escape" into deep water and can't be reached by melee. Mitigation:
  ranged units already shoot into water; AA and artillery fine.
- **Swimming physics**: vanilla land mobs in deep water float (FloatGoal), drift with currents and lose air. All
  Tidewrought units: `canBreatheUnderwater() = true`, no drowning; check every unit keeps `FloatGoal` so they move on
  the surface (RoN's path follower assumes a surface path).
- **WaterAnimal bodies** (Lobster) suffocate on land and get `AQUATIC` from `MobilityClass.of`; override both.
- **Building on water**: placement requires solid ground except bridges. Docks reuse `AbstractBridge` placement; a
  dock on a tidepool must be removed (or become a ruin) when the pool expires - simplest: docks may only sit on
  real water or on solid ground with a tidepool, and they don't depend on the pool after completion.
- **Stale walkability**: real-water edits are rare; tidepool churn is covered by "walkable for everyone" (3.5).
- **Fog / vision over water**: none special. Seagull flyers use the existing flyer sight rules.
- **Maps without water**: everything in slices 1-2 must be playable on a dry map; real water only multiplies value.
  Every Tidewrought GameTest runs in the dry ARENA template.

## 7. Other risks
- **Tidepool abuse**: pools are walkable for everyone and replaceable, so they can't wall a choke or block building
  placement. Placement refuses building footprints and metal patches. Caps in 3.4.
- **Boarding Crew capture** changes building ownership - touches building save data, team colours, production
  queues. Scope carefully or cut.
- **Fall-through**: grep `Factions\.(VILLAGERS|MONSTERS|PIGLINS|VERDANT_COURT)` before merging slice 1; the remaining
  hard-coded lists (GameTests, `BotServerEvents` parse, skirmish names) need Tidewrought added.
- **Lobby width** with six tiles: the name column loses another 22 px; check at GUI scale 3 / 1280 px. Plan a
  dropdown before faction 6.
- **AM entity AI/sounds**: AM mobs carry their own goals, sounds, drops and breeding; each body must clear them (the
  Stag Lancer is the reference).
- **Saves**: tidepools are not saved (scheduled-tick fail-safe removes leftovers); buildings need `BuildingSaveData`
  cases.
