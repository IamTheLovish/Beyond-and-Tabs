# Art fault list - check every render against this before lovish sees it

How to use: before a building, unit, texture or statue goes to lovish, a reviewer (a separate agent that sees only
the images and this list) marks each line pass/fail on the stills - RTS camera (~36 px per block), RTS close-up,
first-person 3/4, dusk (eyes `structure` lane or the preview tools). Fix every fail first. lovish then judges taste
only. **Every fault he catches is added here** with the date and his words, so it is never shown to him twice.

## Buildings
- [ ] **Reads as a mountain / blob, not architecture** - one colour, no silhouette breaks (Oct 19, the classical
      capitol v1: "a white mountain"). Needs clear masses: towers, roofs, walls, voids, shadow lines.
- [ ] **Not cohesive** - parts from different styles or palettes on one building or one faction (Oct 19: "the
      buildings look odd and aren't very cohesive").
- [ ] **Towers not realistic** - no taper, no string courses, roofs that do not overhang, same width top to bottom,
      repeated identical storeys (Oct 20: "the towers don't look realistic enough").
- [ ] **Too small / too plain for its importance** - a capitol must out-detail everything around it (Oct 19:
      "capitals can be a lot more intricate and detailed, can be sized up even more").
- [ ] **Doesn't match the reference** - compared side by side with the reference image he gave, the massing, palette
      or ornament density is off (Oct 20: "imitate the picture more", "your not adding the level of detail and
      sophistication this game needs").
- [ ] **Flat walls** - no depth layering (buttresses, window recesses, trim, frames) at RTS distance.
- [ ] **No light** - no warm windows or lanterns to read detail at dusk (Oct 20, the lit cottage reference: "a good
      way to use light to create detail").
- [ ] **Blocky stacked-block figures** - statues or creatures built from blocks instead of drawn models (Oct 20: "the
      angel looks bad").
- [ ] **Units trapped** - a walkable space under 4 wide x 8 tall that is not sealed; large units can't leave their lab.
- [ ] **Renders wrong from the RTS camera** - missing faces, LOD pops, parts drawn as flat panels (Oct 19 playtest).
- [ ] **Water during construction** - fluids placed before their basin (extractor leak, fixed Oct 20 - keep it fixed).

## Textures
- [ ] **Pepper dots** - high-contrast single-pixel noise on stone, granite, bark or leaves; must read calm and
      cohesive at RTS scale (Oct 20: "the granite looks like it has dots of pepper on it").
- [ ] **Not realistic enough** - compared with his realistic-pack references, colour variation is noisy instead of
      soft and large-scale.

## Trees and nature
- [ ] **Blob canopies / stiff trunks** - trees need articulated branches, layered leaf masses and roots (Oct 19: "I
      like the trees but they could be more realistic looking and articulated better").

## Models (units, statues)
- [ ] **Crude face** - box brow and nose, no readable features at close range (angel v1, Oct 20 self-review).
- [ ] **Stiff columnar cloth** - robes without folds or flow (angel v1).
- [ ] **Gaps between parts** - daylight through feathers, plates or joints (angel v1); measure, don't just look.
- [ ] **Edge-on at the RTS angle** - wings, sails or banners that vanish from the default camera.
- [ ] **Change not visible in game** - a level-up or animation difference that can't be seen at RTS distance (angel
      v1: L1 vs L6 wings look the same).
- [ ] **Reign of Nether look** - any unit, building, icon or name players would recognise from Reign of Nether (Oct 20:
      "I don't want Reign of the Nether in the game").
