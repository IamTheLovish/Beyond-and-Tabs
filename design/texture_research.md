# Texture packs, entity models and shaders: research for Beyond and Tabs

Date: 2026-10-12. Research only. Nothing was downloaded or installed. Every download listed here still needs lovish's approval and a SHA1/SHA512 check before it goes into the instance or the `.mrpack`.

Target: Forge 47.4.0 on MC 1.20.1, with Embeddium 0.3.31 already in `pack/instance_mods.json`. The game is played from a top-down RTS camera plus an optional first-person hero mode. Testers use Windows and Apple Silicon Macs.

## 0. Ground rules that shape every choice

- **How a Modrinth pack is checked.** Every file in `modrinth.index.json` must carry SHA1 **and** SHA512, and download URLs must be on `cdn.modrinth.com`, `github.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com` or `gitlab.com` ([format spec](https://docs.modrinth.com/modpacks/format)). This matches how our `instance_mods.json` already records hashes.
- **Referencing a file vs. redistributing it.** When a project is hosted on Modrinth, the `.mrpack` only *references* the file on Modrinth's CDN, so we never redistribute it. Anything not hosted on Modrinth has to be bundled as an override. That needs a licence that allows redistribution, or the author's explicit permission ([Modrinth modpack rules](https://modrinth.com/news/article/modpack-changes)). Several packs below are "All Rights Reserved" but say modpacks may *include* them. That is fine for a referenced file. Bundling them as an override is not.
- **What a pack can and cannot change about our units.** Our soldiers are drawn by baking vanilla `ModelLayers.PLAYER`, `PLAYER_INNER_ARMOR` and `PLAYER_OUTER_ARMOR`, with skins sliced from our own atlas (`mod/src/main/java/dev/beyondtabs/mod/client/VanillaUnits.java`). Texture packs therefore change terrain, items and armour textures, not our unit skins. A model-replacement mod (EMF) *can* change the player model, so it may change our units. See section 2.
- **A Forge-native connected-textures mod is already installed.** `athena-forge-1.20.1-3.1.2.jar` ships with Chipped. Athena uses its own format, not OptiFine's ([Athena](https://modrinth.com/mod/athena-ctm): MIT; Fabric/Forge/NeoForge; 1.20–1.20.2).

## 1. Resource packs and visual mods for terrain and scenery

How each item reads from the RTS camera is the deciding factor. Top-down, the player mostly sees block **tops** at 1–4 px per texel, and mipmapping averages the detail away. The things that matter most are colour separation between grass, path, stone and water, and contrast against faction colours. Noisy, high-detail textures turn into mush.

## 2. More detailed units: EMF/ETF/CEM vs. our own renderers

**Recommendation: build unit detail in our own renderer. Treat EMF/ETF as a risk, not a tool.**

- **What EMF/ETF are.** EMF re-implements OptiFine's Custom Entity Models (`.jem` files): a resource pack replaces the model of an entity whose renderer is built from vanilla `ModelPart` layers. ETF adds random, emissive and blinking textures ([EMF](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features/version/38Yn9SZ2), [ETF](https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures/version/VzqvqFHV)). Both are client-only, LGPL-3.0, and have Forge 1.20.1 builds (links above). Fresh Animations is the flagship pack built on them.
- **Why they're a hazard for us.** Our units *are* the vanilla player model (`bakeLayer(ModelLayers.PLAYER)`). An EMF pack that touches the player model would restyle or re-animate **every soldier of every faction**. Fresh Animations' Player Extension is one such pack: it requires EMF+ETF and is meant to re-animate players ([Player Extension](https://modrinth.com/project/TAIMVZCL)). That could break our walk/attack timing, hit-boxes-vs-visuals, and team-colour layers. **If EMF ever ships, test that no `player.jem` is active, or have our renderer bypass EMF.**
- **What's realistic in-house** (no new dependencies, all GPL-3 code and our own art):
  1. **Per-faction models.** Register our own `ModelLayerLocation`s with custom `LayerDefinition`s, for example Sunforged pauldrons and halo crest, Ironhide tusks and fur mantle, Verdant antler or bark plates, Tidewrought tricorn and brass gear. The vendored Reign-of-Nether code under `bar/.../unit/modelling/models/` already does exactly this (RoyalGuardModel, NecromancerModel, WraithModel and others), so the pattern is proven on 1.20.1.
  2. **Higher-resolution skins.** Our atlas slices are 64×64 per unit. Moving to 128×128 for big or T3 units doubles detail and costs almost nothing.
  3. **Emissive layers in code.** Gravebound soul-fire eyes, Stormcaller runes and Brassworks furnace glow can use a full-bright render layer (vanilla `RenderType.eyes`-style). This gives ETF's emissive effect without the mod.
  4. **Zoom-based detail levels.** Past a set camera height, drop held items, armour layers and particles, and draw a bolder silhouette plus a team-colour ring or icon instead. Beyond All Reason relies on strategic icons at zoom-out, which helps readability more than any texture pack.
  5. **Optional moddability later.** Because our layers would be real `ModelLayerLocation`s, players *could* reskin units with EMF `.jem` packs if they choose. We just don't depend on it.
- **Authoring:** Blockbench can export Java `LayerDefinition`s. That fits the in-house path with no runtime library.

## 3. Shaders: Oculus on Forge 1.20.1 + Embeddium

- **Status.** Oculus (Iris fork for Forge) is LGPL-3.0. The latest 1.20.1 file is **mc1.20.1-1.8.0, released 2024-12-24**, and the project shows no updates since ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/oculus)). Earlier 1.20.1 builds say outright that they track Embeddium releases, for example "Support for latest Embeddium 0.3.3" ([1.6.15a](https://modrinth.com/mod/GchcoXML/version/Bj8M79wL), [1.6.15](https://modrinth.com/project/GchcoXML/version/KexKV6k0)). Expect version pinning against Embeddium 0.3.31. For NeoForge 1.21+, the Oculus/Embeddium pairing has been retired in favour of Iris (third-party guide: [craftdownunder](https://craftdownunder.co/guides/mods/oculus)), so this is a dead-end branch.
- **Readability from the RTS camera (critical view):**
  - Shadow maps darken exactly where units stand: forests, castle walls, unit blobs. Dark factions (Gravebound, Ironhide) disappear into shadow. Shadow range is limited, so at high camera altitude the shadow edge visibly sweeps across the battlefield.
  - Bloom, volumetric fog, depth of field and motion blur all blur small units at a distance. Depth of field in particular is wrong for a top-down view.
  - Time-of-day lighting makes night battles unreadable unless the pack is tuned.
  - Our own overlays (selection circles, team-colour layers, fog-of-war mixins in `bar/.../mixin/fogofwar/`, build ghosts) are custom render types. Oculus' Forge render-type support was still being fixed in 2024 ([changelog](https://modrinth.com/project/GchcoXML/version/KexKV6k0)). Expect overlays drawn in the wrong pass, invisible, or unlit.
- **Performance.** The shadow pass re-renders terrain and **every entity** a second time. With 100+ units, that roughly doubles entity cost (EMF even added an option to skip animation in the shadow pass for this reason: [changelog](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features/version/38Yn9SZ2)).
- **Apple Silicon.** macOS caps OpenGL at 4.1 ([MacCraft notes](https://modrinth.com/mod/maccraft)), and Sodium/Iris-class mods are known to underperform on Macs for that reason ([Iris discussion #2366](https://github.com/orgs/IrisShaders/discussions/2366)). There are open macOS-specific Iris bugs on 1.20.1, for example M2 Pro + Distant Horizons not rendering with shaders on ([Iris #2521](https://github.com/IrisShaders/Iris/issues/2521)). MakeUp's page lists testing on Windows and Linux only ([MakeUp](https://modrinth.com/shader/makeup-ultra-fast-shaders)).
- **If we offer shaders at all:** make them opt-in and off by default. Use a lightweight pack such as **MakeUp – Ultra Fast** (LGPL-3.0-or-later, Iris/OptiFine, covers 1.20.x, "Potato/Low" tags, shadows and depth of field can be disabled: [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/shader/makeup-ultra-fast-shaders)), with shadows and depth of field off. **Complementary Reimagined** is listed as a custom/ARR licence ([mirror](https://minecraftbible.com/shaders/complementary-reimagined); official page [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-reimagined)). We could not read its licence text during this research, so don't list it until someone has read it.
- **A cheaper way to get the "pretty" effect:** in-house tweaks. Warmer, per-biome fog colour, a faint height tint, and our own top-down ambient-occlusion pass are all controllable and don't need Oculus.

## 4. Ranked shortlist (max 5)

1. **BetterGrassify 1.4.4+forge.1.20.1** (Apache-2.0). It's cheap, Forge-native, has no dependencies, and improves terrain continuity with no readability downside. *Risk:* untested with Embeddium 0.3.31 and Athena. *Needs lovish's approval:* the download, plus hash verification of the Modrinth file.
2. **Faithful 32x (1.20.1 file)** (Faithful License). It's sharper in hero mode and keeps the vanilla palette, so RTS contrast is unchanged. *Risk:* 4x texture memory, and CTM extras won't work (no OptiFine-format support). The licence requires visible credit and a link to faithfulpack.net in our pack description. *Needs approval:* the download, the credit wording, and whether it's on by default or optional.
3. **Excalibur + Clarent 1.20.1 build** (ARR). This is the best thematic match for a fantasy RTS, and Epic Knights support packs exist. *Risks:* dark palette contrast for Gravebound and Ironhide (do an A/B zoom test first). The licence forbids alteration. Excalibur's Modrinth hosting is unverified: if it's CurseForge-only, it can't be referenced in a `.mrpack` without Maffhew's written permission. *Needs approval:* the download, the test, and possibly contacting the author.
4. **Falling Leaves (Forge) 1.20.1-2.1.2** (LGPL-3.0). Adds atmosphere in hero mode. *Risk:* particle clutter over units from the RTS camera, and extra particle cost in big forests. Ship it only if the spawn rate can be capped, or have our camera code disable it at RTS zoom. *Needs approval:* the download and the decision on default on/off.
5. **Oculus 1.8.0 + MakeUp – Ultra Fast**, opt-in only, never default. Some testers will ask for shaders, and this is the lightest legal pair. *Risks:* broken custom overlays (selection, fog of war, team colour), roughly 2x entity cost from the shadow pass, a weak and buggy Mac experience, and a stalled project (last update 2024-12). *Needs approval:* everything, plus a dedicated test on one Windows and one Apple Silicon machine before it's even listed as optional.

**Deliberately not shortlisted:** Fresh Animations/EMF/ETF (tiny gain for ambient mobs, and real risk of restyling our player-model units), Continuity (needs the Sinytra Connector Fabric layer on Forge), Better Leaves (hides units under canopy), Bare Bones (no verified 1.20.1 build).

**Bigger lever than any pack:** per-faction `LayerDefinition` models, 128×128 skins for big units, code-driven emissive layers, and zoom-based detail/icons (section 2). That's what will make units look intricate *and* stay readable.
