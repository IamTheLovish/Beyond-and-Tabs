# Beyond and Tabs - Realistic (prototype, round 13)

Original block textures for the realistic look, generated by `tools/gen_blocks.py` (procedural, deterministic) -
nothing here is copied, traced or upscaled from another pack. 128 texels per block with LabPBR 1.3 maps
(`_n` normal + AO + height, `_s` smoothness / F0 / porosity) for shader packs such as Complementary through Oculus;
without a shader pack only the colour textures are used.

The textures are not stored in git (the repo ignores `*.png`): run `python3 tools/gen_blocks.py` to write them into
this folder. The eyes workflow does that before every tour and fails if the pack comes out empty.

Calibrated against numbers measured from a reference pack (`tools/pack_study.py`, numbers only): brightness, spread,
saturation and mean surface tilt per material, measured at the scale the RTS camera samples. Covers grass (top, side,
side overlay), dirt, stone, cobblestone, sand, gravel, oak log (side, top), oak planks, and (round 3, the blocks the
RTS view shows most after grass and water) stone bricks, polished andesite, smooth stone (+ slab side) and spruce
planks, and (round 5) the leaves of oak, birch, spruce, acacia, jungle and dark oak (with subsurface scattering for
shader packs), and (rounds 6 and 9) still and flowing water: 32 animated frames each of drifting swell and ripples (streaks running downstream on the flow) that loop seamlessly, and (round 7) raw iron (the metal resource patches), gold block, barrel and hay bale, and (round 8) the bark and end grain of spruce, birch, acacia, jungle and dark oak, and (round 11) the other factions' building blocks: polished blackstone (+ bricks), deepslate (bricks,
tiles, plain, top, polished, cobbled), cut and oxidized copper, azalea leaves (+ flowering), netherrack, nether bricks,
prismarine bricks, dark prismarine, moss and mossy stone bricks, and (round 12) sandstone, terracotta, polished basalt, bone block, quartz, and the
terrain of other biomes: andesite, granite, diorite, calcite, tuff, snow, podzol, coarse dirt, clay, red sand, mud, and (round 13) the planks and stripped logs of every wood, polished granite and diorite,
blackstone, obsidian, red nether bricks, chiseled stone bricks and the dirt path. Part of Beyond and Tabs (GPL-3, see the repo's LICENSE).
